# Naming, Legal, and Policy Gates

## 1. Working-title warning

`Call On` is a codename, not a cleared production brand. There are existing products using similar names, including a calling application. This handoff makes no trademark-availability claim.

Before public launch:

- run USPTO and relevant state/international searches;
- search app stores, domains, social handles, and common-law use;
- review confusingly similar marks in software, community, lending, and telecommunications;
- engage qualified trademark counsel;
- choose and register domain/handles only after risk review;
- update app identifiers, sender names, privacy/terms, and visual assets.

Codex should centralize working name in configuration and avoid scattering it through business logic.

## 2. Legal documents

Counsel must review:

- Terms of Service;
- Privacy Policy;
- community/member rules;
- prohibited/restricted categories;
- borrower/lender acknowledgement;
- moderation and appeal process;
- liability allocation/waiver limitations;
- electronic communications and channel consent;
- child/minor policy;
- data retention/deletion;
- incident and law-enforcement request procedure;
- applicable accessibility/consumer/privacy obligations.

Do not copy generic marketplace terms. The product is community coordination with physical-risk implications.

## 3. Age policy

Recommended P0: accounts and commitments limited to adults 18+. Children may be beneficiaries of an event but should not create accounts, accept custody, or be publicly profiled. Counsel must confirm.

## 4. Insurance and liability

P0 does not claim insurance coverage. Do not market a disclaimer as eliminating liability. Before allowing moderate/high-risk categories at scale, review:

- platform/business liability;
- owner/borrower responsibility;
- HOA/community relationship;
- whether any commercial rental classification is triggered;
- coverage exclusions;
- incident documentation;
- jurisdiction-specific enforceability.

## 5. HOA/community positioning

Avoid implying that an HOA guarantees, owns, inspects, or endorses personal items unless it formally does. Circle admin tools do not make the HOA a party to each exchange by default. Contract and onboarding language must reflect the actual relationship.

## 6. Privacy/legal gate

Map data categories, processors, retention, access, deletion, and incident response. Determine applicable laws from actual users/jurisdictions. Do not make unsupported compliance badges or statements.

## 7. Launch decision record

Before production, create an approved record containing:

- final name/legal entity;
- counsel and review date;
- launch jurisdictions;
- age policy;
- prohibited categories;
- terms/privacy versions;
- insurer/broker outcome;
- moderator escalation owner;
- data retention schedule;
- unresolved risks consciously accepted.
