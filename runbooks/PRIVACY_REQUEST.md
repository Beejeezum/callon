# Privacy Request

1. Authenticate requester with step-up verification.
2. Record request type and jurisdiction; route to privacy owner/counsel.
3. Inventory profile, contacts, memberships, Asks, Offers, messages, Resources, media, incidents, analytics identifiers, and provider records.
4. Preserve records legally required for safety, disputes, accounting, security, or consent evidence; explain exceptions.
5. Export in a readable structured format without exposing other users' private data.
6. Delete/anonymize according to approved retention policy and provider processes.
7. Record completion without storing the exported personal content in the ticket.
