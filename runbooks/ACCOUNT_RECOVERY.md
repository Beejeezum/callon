# Account Recovery and Contact Change

1. Prefer recovery through an already verified alternate contact/session.
2. Require step-up verification for phone/email changes.
3. Never allow Circle admins to reset a user's authentication factor.
4. For manual recovery, require two trained operators, minimum evidence, and an audited case.
5. Do not reveal whether a phone/email has an account before verification.
6. Revoke existing sessions after sensitive recovery; notify old and new verified contacts.
7. Provide a cooling-off period before exact-location access or high-impact admin actions when risk warrants.
