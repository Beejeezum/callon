# Moderation and Dispute

## Principles

Private, scoped, factual, reversible where possible, and separate from public reputation.

## Flow

1. Reporter selects a concrete category and supplies minimum necessary facts.
2. Preserve Loan/Commitment/Audit records; do not expose the report publicly.
3. Give the other party a private response opportunity unless safety requires containment.
4. Moderator accesses messages/location/evidence only through a scoped, expiring, audited grant.
5. Resolve with documented factual outcome: return arranged, component missing, damage acknowledged, policy violation, no finding, account restriction, or escalation.
6. Never publish an accusation, score, or permanent badge.
7. High-risk injury, criminal allegation, child safety, discrimination, or legal demand escalates to the designated human/legal process.
